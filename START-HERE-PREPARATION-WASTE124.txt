INCLUDED
Inventory > Preparation & Costs: all ingredient quantities, usable cooked output,
total worker minutes, hourly labor cost, fuel/electricity and other cooking costs.
A separate estimated percentage mode does not also add the actual cooking costs.
Inventory > Wastage: raw or cooked food, grams/kilograms, milliliters/liters,
the item's own stock unit, or packs with declared contents. Cooked waste uses the
selected batch's full cost. The ledger separates ingredient loss from cooking loss.
Correcting a mistaken waste entry adds an audited negative waste record and restores
stock once. It is for recording errors, not for reclaiming food actually discarded.
Cooking batches consume raw Inventory and add cooked Inventory in one transaction.
Existing POS ingredient links then consume the cooked stock when a menu is paid.
POS pending offline payments reserve local ingredient quantities. Late account
responses are rejected, and permission errors cannot turn into offline fallback.

SETUP OF COOKED FOOD
1. Create a separate Inventory item for cooked filling/sauce/etc. Choose its stock
   unit, e.g. kg. Do not use the raw ingredient's Inventory item as cooked output.
2. Link every raw ingredient to Inventory in POS Management > Ingredient links.
   Recipe g to Inventory kg uses factor 0.001.
3. In Preparation & Costs record the raw and added ingredients, cooking costs and
   actual usable output. Use all worker minutes (two people x 30 minutes = 60).
4. Create/link the cooked ingredient to that cooked Inventory item, and use it in
   the menu recipe. Existing linked ingredient records receive the batch's
   ingredient-only cost and a separate full cooking cost. If creating the cooked
   ingredient AFTER the first batch, enter its ingredient cost and link first;
   the next recorded batch refreshes the stored costs. Do not copy labor-inclusive
   cost into an ingredient-only margin calculation.
5. A 100 g portion linked to stock in kg uses quantity 100 and factor 0.001.
6. Record actual cooked waste against its actual batch. The batch's prices are
   frozen even if future ingredient prices change. Sale prices are not changed.

OFFLINE CAPTURE
Open and successfully load POS and Preparation & Waste online on each device.
Open the cashier shift online before going offline. A successful local save means
the IndexedDB transaction committed. Pending records remain until server receipt.
Keep the same account. Reconnect and use Refresh & sync to submit preparation/waste.
Changed ingredient versions, insufficient stock or lost permissions stop replay;
they do not silently discard the captured record. After reconnecting, Remove local
capture first checks whether it was already recorded on the server. A failed local
capture can then be removed and re-entered with reviewed information.

LIMITATIONS TO RESOLVE BEFORE DAILY RELEASE
The existing sign-in still needs the server after a cold offline reload. This draft
does NOT add an offline sign-in bypass. Cached, already-established sessions can
capture locally. Full offline cold start, essential asset verification and weak
network deadlines are part of the next phase.
Cooked stock is pooled across batches; the operator identifies the discarded batch.
Sales do not yet allocate lots by FIFO/expiry. Prepared-input costs use the saved
full cost on the ingredient record, so exact lot costing for nested preparations
requires batch allocation. Latest batch costs refresh recipe costing; historic
batch waste costs remain frozen.
Preparation batch corrections do not yet have an atomic reversal workflow. Do not
reverse only one of its movements; linked preparation/waste movements are blocked
from the generic Reverse action. Waste corrections have their own audited workflow.
This is an operational loss report. Allocated payroll/fuel are existing expenses;
do not post the full loss on top of them as another cash expense automatically.
Inventory/kitchen list-history downloads still need pagination. POS snapshot and
receipt/report paging have been improved in update 125 (apply migrations 10–11).
Offline POS replay still validates against current recipes; recipe-version review
and reconciliation after changes need strengthening before daily offline operation.
Separate devices can oversell while disconnected; use one offline selling till or
implement per-device stock allocations. An offline phone cannot see a new order.

ORDER NOTIFICATIONS — NEXT PHASE
Save the public order once on the server. Commit an alert outbox with that order.
Deliver independently to POS, phone web push and opted-in WhatsApp recipients.
Store delivery attempts separately from order state. Order accepted by staff is
an explicit action shared by all devices, not a synonym for message delivered/read.
Use server retries and show unacknowledged orders. Keep public product IDs and POS
menu/choice IDs mapped, validate prices on the server, and prevent duplicate orders.
An internet-connected phone on mobile data can serve as backup for weak restaurant
Wi-Fi. Automatic WhatsApp alerts need Business Platform setup, a sender, applicable
templates, consented recipients, credentials and delivery webhooks. No messages
were sent and no phone recipients were assumed in this draft.

REPOSITORY RECOMMENDATION
Keep Restaurant Back Office in its existing repository. Add a lightweight /pos/
entry and its own asset loading path. Repository count does not determine runtime
speed. Do not clone ingredient/menu records into a second POS database.
