# Restaurant release 133

The previous login styling lost to an older, more specific 360px rule. This release uses the complete current Accounting desktop login structure and scoped styles (reference commit 8d0ab9430ba70a03e79394c0bbc50474c0b63861): 430px card, 28px desktop padding, 52px logo, 43px fields, 32px desktop actions, original typography, colors and circular loading feedback. Phone controls retain 44px targets. Credential submission is disabled until the sign-in handler is installed.

The desktop/tablet banner and account actions now share one header. Its title is shown once; quotations and their settings controls are removed. Phone banners stay hidden. Integration uses a search field, ingredient dropdown and one inline stock-link form, with a readable conversion and links to recipe portions/costing. Archive records are excluded from working lists but remain available in Menu Display and the ingredient archive. POS opens only the live shared catalog; old saved sample preferences are ignored.

A guarded, idempotent migration archives the confirmed September 27 sample seed by exact code, name and creation timestamp. It preserves recipes, costs, identities and sales history; excludes public records and newly created recipes; and retains ingredients used by real recipes or preparation formulas. Live result: 20 sample menus and 19 unused sample ingredients archived, leaving 34 current menus and 64 ingredients. Public records retain their before/after fingerprint, with zero shared-name or price mismatches.
